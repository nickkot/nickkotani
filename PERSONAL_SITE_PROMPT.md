# Personal Site Interview Prompt

Paste everything below the line into Claude (or Claude Code, from inside this repo) to start building the site.

---

You're going to help me build my personal website. Before you write any code, interview me so the site actually sounds and looks like me.

## How to run the interview

- Ask **one question at a time** (or a small group of 2–3 closely related ones). Wait for my answer before moving on.
- Keep questions short and conversational. Offer examples or multiple-choice options when a question is open-ended, but always let me answer freely.
- If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up to sharpen it, then move on.
- I can say **"skip"** to any question. Pick a sensible default and note that you did.
- I can say **"done"** at any time to jump straight to the summary.
- Let me know which section we're in as we go (for example, "Section 3/7: Work & projects").

## What to cover, in order

1. **Basics.** My name as it should appear, a one-line description of what I do, and where I'm based (if I want that shown).
2. **Purpose & audience.** Why I want this site (job hunting, portfolio, freelance clients, blog, a home on the internet), who should visit it, and the one thing a visitor should do or remember.
3. **About me.** My background, what I'm working on now, what I care about, and a few personal details or interests I'm happy to share.
4. **Work & projects.** 3–6 things to showcase. For each: name, what it is, my role, outcome or impact, and links or images. Ask whether I want a résumé or CV page.
5. **Writing & extras.** Whether I want a blog or notes section, a "now" page, talks, photos, a reading list, or anything else.
6. **Contact & links.** Email (public or a form?), GitHub, LinkedIn, X/Bluesky, and others. Which ones matter most.
7. **Look & feel.** 2–3 sites I like and why, mood words (minimal, playful, editorial, techy, warm…), color or font preferences, light/dark mode, and anything I definitely don't want.
8. **Technical setup.** Whether I have a domain, where I'd like to host it (GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify…), whether I prefer plain HTML/CSS or a framework (Astro, Next.js, Hugo…), and how comfortable I am editing it myself later.

## When the interview is finished

1. Give me a **concise summary** of everything you learned, as a site brief: goals, audience, pages, content per page, visual direction, and tech stack. Flag any defaults you chose for skipped questions.
2. Propose a **sitemap** and a short **plan** for building it.
3. Ask me to confirm or correct the brief. **Don't start building until I say go.**
4. Once I approve, build the site in this repo with clean, well-organized code, real content from my answers (no lorem ipsum), a responsive layout, good accessibility, and a README that explains how to edit content and deploy.

Start now with your first question.
